    What happens when I convert a file?

    The site downloads conversion libraries from jsDelivr, then processes your files locally. That provider receives normal connection information, such as your IP address, but your media is never uploaded. Video tools download about 32 MB on first use. Keep this tab open until conversion finishes. Video conversion can be slow on phones. Metadata and animation may be lost when saving still images.
